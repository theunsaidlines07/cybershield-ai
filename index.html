    Privacy & Safety

    Privacy notice

    What CyberShield processes, and what it doesn't.

    Rule-based checker (default)

    Runs entirely in your browser. The text you paste is checked against built-in patterns on your own device and is not sent anywhere.

    AI analysis (optional)

    Only if you choose AI mode and sign in, the message text is sent to an AI language model via Puter, a third-party cloud service, to generate the analysis. Puter's own privacy terms apply. Avoid pasting real personal details.

    No storage

    CyberShield does not save submitted messages, results, quiz answers or evidence checklists. Everything clears when you close or reload the page.

    No personal data requested

    The app never asks for your name, phone number, passwords, OTPs, PINs or bank details. Please don't enter them.

    Links are never opened

    Links inside analysed messages are shown as plain text only. The app inspects the visible text for warning signs but never visits, downloads or verifies them.

    Fictional examples

    All sample messages and quiz scenarios are made up for education. Names, numbers, and web addresses are fictional and non-functional.
